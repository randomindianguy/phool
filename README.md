# Memory wall prototype

Question: after the illustrated door opens, how should the large central picture
show a swivelling 360-degree collection of the photos present in the physical
display?

The cottage room and four local-video frames stay the same. Only the central
photo experience changes:

- A: a draggable 3D orbit of photo cards.
- B: a horizontal panoramic photo wall.
- C: a rotating ring with one enlarged focal photo.

All people, photos, audio, and videos are placeholders. The four small wall
frames will later point at local MP4 files. The golden thread provides the idle
click cue.
